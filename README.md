KODEK is made for the fun of modding games, the freedom to experiment,  
change things up, and turn your game into your own personal playground.

It’s about giving you more ways to play. Tweak the experience, mess around with different features,  
push the game beyond normal gameplay, or just cause some chaos and have fun with it.

KODEK isn’t about completely removing every limitation.  
It’s about giving you more control over your experience and more freedom to enjoy games the way you want.

### Play normally when you want to. Change the rules when you don’t.
